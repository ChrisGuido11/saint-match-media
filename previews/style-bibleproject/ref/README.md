# Style reference: BibleProject "Angel of the Lord" (comparison samples only)

Source: BibleProject "The Angel of the Lord" video, shared as an Instagram reel by @legacychurchohio (reel DeCfWjHDbMH).
The BibleProject is the author and owner of these frames. They are here only as a style reference for internal sample stills. Do not reuse them in posts.

- ref-01.jpg to ref-06.jpg: 1920x1080 frames at 63.5s, 79.4s, 95.3s, 127.0s, 174.7s, 206.4s
- contact-sheet.png: 16 evenly spaced frames plus scene cuts

Style notes: flat 2D digital illustration with no outlines, an even fine grain or noise texture over every surface, chunky rounded faceless figures (small heads, closed-crescent or dot eyes, solid blob beards, one flat body color each), warm dusty earth tones (sand, ochre, terracotta, rust, plum) for the human world against deep aubergine purple darkness, and the divine shown as translucent overlapping lavender, periwinkle, pink, peach and white glow shapes with sparkle stars, bokeh capsules and dashed swirl lines. Layered cutout planes with dark silhouette foregrounds, wobbly circle vignette windows on a textured purple field. Motion is 2D cutout animation: parallax drift, morphing glow shapes, slow camera pushes, circular portal transitions.
