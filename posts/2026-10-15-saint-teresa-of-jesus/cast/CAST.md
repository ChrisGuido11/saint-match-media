# Cast

LOCKED LOOK | teresa | olive-tan skin; dark hair; no beard. Secular beats 01, 02, 04, and 08: rust-brown 16th-century dress and a light cloth on the hair. Convent beats 03, 06, and 07: the same face in a dark brown habit, black veil, and white wimple of the Incarnation convent, not the later reform habit. Beat 05 keeps the secular dress at the edge of the street.

The father, the townspeople, Francis Borgia, and Peter of Alcantara are one-offs.
